“In the challenging spinto soprano role of Lady Macbeth, Viktoriia Balan is a revelation” — THE STAGE

Ukrainian lirico-spinto soprano Viktoriia Balan's "glorious voice" and "bright and incisive vocal timbre" have earned her recognition across European stages. She is a finalist and a prize winner of several prestigious international competitions, including 1st Prize at "Spazio Musica" in Orvieto, 2nd Prize at "Franco Bonisolli" in Rovereto, winner of the 78th "European Community" Competition in Spoleto. Her career highlights include UK debut as Lady Macbeth (Macbeth) at Longborough Festival Opera, debut at Teatro Lirico Sperimentale di Spoleto as La Contessa (Le Nozze di Figaro) and Lady Macbeth, and Anna in Zandonai's I cavalieri di Ekebù at Teatro Zandonai in Rovereto.
