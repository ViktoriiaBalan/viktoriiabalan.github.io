Press

Lady Macbeth

Giuseppe Verdi — Macbeth
Longborough Festival Opera, UK (2026)

“In the challenging spinto soprano role of Lady Macbeth, Viktoriia Balan is a revelation. Her forays into the highest registers of her voice are thrilling, and her final 'mad' scene is full of pathos.”

Inge Kjemtrup, The Stage

“This production benefits from some outstanding singers, chief of which is Ukrainian soprano Viktoriia Balan as Lady Macbeth making her Longborough debut. She perfectly matches Verdi's requirement for his femme fatale to have 'the voice of a devil'. She commands the stage and fearlessly dispatches Verdi's formidable challenges, richly dark below the stave and scorching when delivering a high D flat in her 'Una macchia è qui tuttora!' Self-possessed, she bestrides the stage in a black leather coat giving faint hints of a dominatrix, her 'Brindisi' wonderfully rousing.”

David Truslove, Opera Today

“Viktoriia Balan's company debut is a triumph. Lady Macbeth is one of Verdi's most demanding creations, requiring not simply vocal stamina but complete dramatic conviction, and Balan delivers both. The voice combines warmth, richness and thrilling brilliance across its range, but what impresses most is the intelligence behind the singing. Every phrase has dramatic purpose... Balan's performance charts the character's psychological disintegration with unwavering authority. It is the sort of debut that immediately marks out a singer to watch.”

Mike Smith, OperaScene

“Viktoriia Balan's gleefully sadistic Lady Macbeth. It must be one of the most fun roles to play in the canon and Balan lapped up sneer with sinister ease. An electric chemistry with Stone's Macbeth, she feels genuinely dangerous... There is a real focussed power behind the voice coupled with a tactile grasp of the Italian text: a very, very engaging and exciting performance.”

Jonathan Whiting, Opera Now

“As Lady Macbeth, Viktoriia Balan delivers an exceptional performance both vocally and dramatically. She makes one of Verdi's most demanding soprano roles appear deceptively effortless. Ambizioso spirto, with its treacherous coloratura, extreme dynamic contrasts and relentless vocal demands, is sung with complete assurance. The sleepwalking scene proves even more remarkable... Her richly coloured voice and commanding stage presence make for a memorable Lady Macbeth.”

Josi Steinfeld, Plays To See

“Viktoriia Balan was an outstanding Lady Macbeth, who revealed a sumptuous soprano that was possessed of a certain edge that worked perfectly for the role. Her transformation from the person who seemed almost too good at keeping any guilt in check, to one who was completely lost to madness, felt convincing.”

Sam Smith, MusicOMH

“Two rising stars took the plaudits on opening night of Longborough's first-ever production of Verdi's Macbeth. Soprano, Viktoriia Balan made a stunning debut in the role of Lady Macbeth. With her glorious soprano voice she is an instant success... The star, the very talented soprano, Viktoriia Balan, makes a lovely pairing with baritone Stone...”

Clive Peacock, Seen and Heard International

“Viktoriia Balan makes a stirring impression as a forceful Lady Macbeth with an iron will, though also able to modify her temperament convincingly to coax or goad as needs be, demonstrating firmly that she wears the trousers.”

Curtis Rogers, Classical Source

“...with Ukrainian soprano Viktoriia Balan spirited and extremely well sung as Lady Macbeth.”

Mark Ronan, The Article
