About

Viktoriia Balan is a Ukrainian lirico-spinto soprano based in Italy, whose repertoire ranges from the lyricism of Mozart to the dramatic worlds of Verdi, Puccini and Italian verismo, with a particular affinity for the great heroines of the Italian repertoire. Praised for her richly coloured voice, vocal warmth and brilliance, vocal stamina and compelling dramatic presence, she brings both musical intelligence and strong theatrical instinct to her interpretations.

In 2026, Balan made her United Kingdom debut at Longborough Festival Opera as Lady Macbeth in Verdi’s Macbeth, conducted by Nil Venditti and directed by Karolina Sofulak. Her performance was praised for its warmth, richness and thrilling brilliance across the range, as well as for its vocal stamina and dramatic conviction (The Stage). She had previously made her debut in the role at the Teatro Lirico Sperimentale di Spoleto, conducted by Carlo Palleschi in the production of Ugo Giacomazzi and Luigi Di Gangi.

Her recent Italian engagements also include her debut as Anna in Riccardo Zandonai’s I Cavalieri di Ekebù at Teatro Zandonai in Rovereto in April 2026, conducted by Francesco Rosa and directed by Daniele Piscopo, and her debut as the Countess in Mozart’s Le nozze di Figaro at the Teatro Lirico Sperimentale di Spoleto in 2025, conducted by Marco Angius and directed by Henning Brockhaus.

Alongside her operatic career, Balan is active in concert repertoire. In 2025 she appeared as soprano soloist in Rossini’s Stabat Mater with the Orchestra delle Alpi in concerts throughout Trentino. Her concert engagements have also included a Concerto di Pasqua in Spoleto and Orvieto, a Liederabend in Spoleto, and performances at the Teatro Goldoni in Livorno as part of Dancing Mascagni.

Balan’s artistic achievements have been recognised through a number of international competitions. In 2024 she was selected as a winner of the 78th Comunità Europea Competition for Young Opera Singers at the Teatro Lirico Sperimentale di Spoleto. In the same year, she received the First Prize at the Spazio Musica International Competition in Orvieto, followed in 2025 by the Second Prize at the Franco Bonisolli International Opera Singers Competition in Rovereto.

Her musical education began in Ukraine. In 2020 she graduated from the Odesa National A. V. Nezhdanova Academy of Music with a Master’s degree in Opera Singing and Vocal Pedagogy. After moving to Italy in 2022, she continued her artistic development at the Mascagni Academy in Livorno, where she worked with Franco Vassallo and Jennifer Hamilton. She subsequently took part in masterclasses with Daniela Barcellona and Alessandro Vitiello at the Teatro Lirico Giuseppe Verdi in Trieste, and with Raina Kabaivanska in Sofia.

During her training at the Teatro Lirico Sperimentale di Spoleto, Balan had the opportunity to work with distinguished singers and teachers including Nicola Ulivieri, Marina Comparato, Carmela Remigio, Alda Caiello and Sara Mingardo.

Her repertoire includes Lady Macbeth in Verdi’s Macbeth, the Countess in Mozart’s Le nozze di Figaro, Anna in Zandonai’s I Cavalieri di Ekebù, Santuzza in Mascagni’s Cavalleria rusticana, Mimì in Puccini’s La bohème, Micaëla in Bizet’s Carmen and Matilde in Mascagni’s Silvano. Her repertoire also includes Leonora in Verdi’s La forza del destino and Il trovatore, Puccini’s Suor Angelica, Madama Butterfly and Tosca.
