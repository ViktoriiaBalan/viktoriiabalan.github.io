Viktoriia Balan

Ukrainian Lirico-Spinto Soprano

Artistic Biography

A Voice of Passion, Drama & Precision

Viktoriia Balan is a celebrated Ukrainian lirico-spinto soprano whose performances are renowned for their technical mastery, profound emotional depth, and commanding stage presence. Currently based in Italy, her vocal versatility spans the rich romantic repertoire of Giuseppe Verdi and Giacomo Puccini to refined sacred works and complex 20th-century opera roles.

Her musical foundation was forged at the prestigious Antonina Nezhdanova National Academy of Music in Odesa, Ukraine, where she obtained her Master’s Degree with honors in Solo Singing and Vocal Pedagogy under the mentorship of Distinguished Artist of Ukraine, Prof. N. S. Yutesh. Seeking to refine her artistry in the cradle of opera, Viktoriia moved to Italy, perfecting her technique at the Conservatorio F.A. Bonporti in Trento under the guidance of Prof. M. L. Sanchez and collaborating with internationally acclaimed vocal coaches across Europe.

Viktoriia’s career is marked by notable triumphs in prestigious international vocal competitions. She gained widespread acclaim after winning the 78th European Community Competition for Young Opera Singers organized by the Teatro Lirico Sperimentale "A. Belli" in Spoleto. She secured First Prize at the XXIX Concorso Lirico Internazionale "Spazio Musica" in Orvieto and won the "Franco Bonisolli" International Opera Competition in Rovereto, alongside accolades at the "Città di Riva del Garda" and "Trofeo La Fenice" competitions.

On the operatic stage, Viktoriia has embodied some of the most demanding soprano roles in the canon. Her performance as Lady Macbeth in Verdi’s Macbeth—presented at the Longborough Festival Opera in the UK as well as in Spoleto—won critical praise for its dramatic intensity and vocal authority. Other signature roles include La Contessa in Mozart's Le nozze di Figaro, Anna in Zandonai's I cavalieri di Ekebù at Teatro Zandonai, and leading roles in contemporary productions such as La Democrazia in Sussulto.

In addition to opera, Viktoriia is an active concert soloist. She has performed Rossini's Stabat Mater and Mozart's Requiem with major orchestras, including the Odesa Philharmonic, and regularly appears in recitals across Italy, Great Britain, Germany, and Switzerland.
