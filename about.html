About

Biography

Viktoriia Balan is a Ukrainian lirico-spinto soprano based in Italy, acclaimed for her rich vocal timbre, dramatic flexibility, and commanding artistic maturity across the Italian romantic and verismo repertoires.

Her operatic career has seen her perform demanding leading roles on notable international stages. She performed as Lady Macbeth in Verdi's Macbeth at the Teatro Lirico Sperimentale di Spoleto "A. Belli" in Italy and at the Longborough Festival Opera in the United Kingdom. Her operatic portrayals also include the lead role in the contemporary opera La Democrazia in Sussulto in Spoleto, La Contessa in Mozart's Le nozze di Figaro at Teatro Mancinelli in Orvieto, Anna in Zandonai's I cavalieri di Ekebù at Teatro Zandonai in Rovereto, and Clotilde in Bellini's Norma at Teatro Sociale di Trento. Earlier in her career, she performed extensively as a soprano soloist in sacred and concert repertoire, including Rossini's Stabat Mater and Mozart's Requiem with the Odesa Philharmonic Society.

Viktoriia's vocal excellence has been recognized with top prizes at major international singing competitions. She emerged as the Winner of the 78th European Community Competition for Young Opera Singers organized by the Teatro Lirico Sperimentale "A. Belli" in Spoleto. Additionally, she was awarded 1st Prize at the XXIX International Opera Competition "Spazio Musica" in Orvieto, 1st Prize at the International Opera Competition "Franco Bonisolli" in Rovereto, Winner of the "Trofeo La Fenice" International Vocal Competition in Seravezza, and 3rd Prize at the Concorso Lirico Internazionale "Città di Riva del Garda".

Her formal musical education spans advanced studies in both Italy and Ukraine. She earned her Master's Degree in Opera Singing and Vocal Pedagogy from the Odesa National Academy of Music in Odesa, Ukraine. After moving to Italy, she continued her education at Masterclasses with Daniela Barcellona and Alessandro Vitiello, Raina Kabaivansa and Mascagni Academy.
